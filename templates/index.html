Under the Hood

How our model catches deepfakes

Instead of relying solely on raw video appearance, we fuse three complementary forensic signals.

🧬

Skin Micro-Texture (LBP)

Generative AI often over-smoothes human pores and fine wrinkles, giving faces a plastic sheen. Local Binary Patterns catch this micro-texture loss.

📐

Boundary Margin (20%)

Face-swaps must blend a new face onto the host's head. By expanding facial bounding boxes by 20%, we directly inspect blending seams around the jawline and ears.

⚡

Frequency Signatures (DCT)

Up-sampling in neural networks leaves behind periodic mathematical noise in frequency space invisible to the human eye, but unmistakable to Discrete Cosine Transforms.
